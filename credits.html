  • Open Food Facts. Food search and barcode results come from Open Food Facts, made available under the Open Database License. Individual contents are under the Database Contents License, and product photos are by Open Food Facts contributors under CC BY-SA 3.0. Brand names, logos and packaging shown in photos belong to their owners. Searches you've made are kept on your device to make the app faster and work offline.
  • Everyday foods. The built-in list of everyday foods uses typical values per 100 g, based on public food composition data such as USDA FoodData Central (public domain) and the UK's Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0). Values are rounded averages, so they won't match every brand.
