Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: NextForm keeps your training and food log on your device. If you sign in with Google, it also keeps a private backup in your account so you can use it on other devices. Nobody else can see your data. There are no adverts, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Who we are

NextForm is run by Marley Harris, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: Nextformstudio@outlook.com.

What NextForm stores

  • Your profile: name, age, height, the strength comparison you choose, goal weight, weekly goal and an optional profile photo.
  • Your training: workouts, sets, distances, times, effort, notes, plans you create, and the levels and quests worked out from them.
  • Your body weight entries.
  • Your food log: foods, amounts, calories and macros, your daily targets and saved meals.
  • If you sign in: your Google account email address and an account ID from Google. NextForm doesn't receive your Google password.

Body weight, food and training details can count as health data. NextForm only uses them to show your own progress, grades and targets back to you.

Where it's kept

  • On your device, in your browser's storage. If you use NextForm as a guest, this is the only copy. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Export backup in Profile to keep a copy.
  • In your NextForm backup, if you sign in with Google. This is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), and Google handles it on our behalf as a data processor. Security rules mean only your signed-in account can read or change your backup. Google may store data outside the UK, under its standard data protection terms and safeguards.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • To provide the app you've asked to use, including backups and restoring on other devices (performing our agreement with you).
  • For health-related details such as weight and food: your explicit consent, which you give by choosing to log them. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting the entries or your account.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting a workout or food entry removes it from your backup too. A short record that it was deleted is kept (just its ID), so other devices remove it as well. Delete my account in Profile permanently deletes your backup and your sign-in with NextForm.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • See and take a copy of your data: use Export backup in Profile, which downloads all of it as a file.
  • Correct it: edit your profile, workouts and food entries in the app.
  • Delete it: delete individual entries, or use Delete my account (signed in) or Reset (guest) in Profile.
  • Object or ask a question about how your data is used: email Nextformstudio@outlook.com.

If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

NextForm isn't intended for children under 13. If you're under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before using it, especially the calorie targets.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and your backup can only be read by your signed-in account. Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own security, so keep your phone locked.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app.
